# The Chess Pieces

Chess is played on an 8×8 board. Each side starts with **16 pieces**: 1 King, 1 Queen, 2 Rooks, 2 Bishops, 2 Knights, and 8 Pawns. Here's how each one moves.

---

## ♔ King
**The most important piece — if it's checkmated, you lose.**

- Moves **one square** in any direction (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal)
- Can never move to a square that is under attack
- Special move: [[Castling]]

**Value:** Infinite (priceless — losing it means losing the game)

---

## ♕ Queen
**The most powerful piece on the board.**

- Moves any number of squares in **any direction** — like a Rook and Bishop combined
- Can't jump over pieces
- One Queen controls up to 27 squares from the center!

**Value:** ~9 points

---

## ♖ Rook
- Moves any number of squares **horizontally or vertically**
- Can't jump over pieces
- Pair of Rooks working together are very powerful
- Special move: [[Castling]] (with the King)

**Value:** ~5 points

---

## ♗ Bishop
- Moves any number of squares **diagonally**
- Can't jump over pieces
- Always stays on the **same color** square it started on
- You start with one light-squared Bishop and one dark-squared Bishop
- Two Bishops together ("Bishop pair") are usually a small advantage

**Value:** ~3 points

---

## ♘ Knight
**The trickiest piece — the only one that can jump over other pieces.**

- Moves in an **"L" shape**: two squares in one direction, then one square perpendicular
- Can land on 8 different squares from the center (fewer from the edges)
- Because it jumps, it ignores all pieces between start and destination
- Great for forking (attacking two pieces at once — see [[Tricks]])

**Value:** ~3 points

> "A Knight on the rim is dim." — Keep Knights near the center, away from the edges.

---

## ♙ Pawn
**8 of them, and they're more important than they look.**

- Moves **forward only**, one square at a time
- On its **very first move**, it can optionally move **two squares** forward
- **Captures diagonally** — one square forward-left or forward-right
- Cannot move backward (ever!)

### Special Pawn Rules:

**En passant**: If your opponent moves a pawn two squares forward (landing beside your pawn), you can capture it *as if* it had only moved one square. This option is only available on the **very next move**.

**Promotion**: If a pawn reaches the **other end of the board** (8th rank for White, 1st rank for Black), it must be promoted to a Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight. Almost always promote to a Queen.

**Value:** ~1 point

---

## Point Values Summary

| Piece | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Queen | 9 |
| Rook | 5 |
| Bishop | 3 |
| Knight | 3 |
| Pawn | 1 |
| King | ∞ |

Use these to decide trades: trading a Bishop (3) for a Rook (5) is a good deal for you (winning a "minor exchange"). Trading a Queen (9) for a Rook (5) is bad.
