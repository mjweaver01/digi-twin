## Recommended Resources

### Free Platforms
- **[Lichess.org](https://lichess.org)** — 100% free, no ads. Great puzzles, lessons, and analysis.
- **[Chess.com](https://chess.com)** — Large community, good lessons (some features paid).

### Learning Path
1. Learn how the pieces move → play some casual games
2. Do **tactics puzzles** every day (even 10/day makes a big difference)
3. Study one opening as White and one as Black — stick with them
4. Review your games using the computer analysis tool

### Books (Optional)
- *Chess Fundamentals* by José Raúl Capablanca — free, a classic
- *How to Reassess Your Chess* by Jeremy Silman — great for improving beyond beginner

## Quick Reference

| Piece | Value | Special |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Queen | 9 | — |
| Rook | 5 | Castles with King |
| Bishop | 3 | Same color always |
| Knight | 3 | Jumps over pieces |
| Pawn | 1 | Promotes, en passant |
| King | ∞ | Castle, never in check |

**Opening rules:** Control center · Develop pieces · Castle · Don't move same piece twice

**Every move ask:** What is my opponent threatening? What's my plan?
