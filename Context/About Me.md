# About Me

My name is Mike (Michael J Weaver). I am 33 years old, based in Columbus, Ohio.

## Professional identity

I am a **frontend engineer** by origin, having progressed from junior to lead UI developer over a decade of professional web work. Most of my experience is in **D2C e-commerce** and **SaaS platforms** — Rogue Fitness, Triple Whale, Shopify apps, and freelance client work.

I am now a **full-stack TypeScript developer**, comfortable across the stack but strongest on the frontend. I have used **Angular, React, and Vue**, along with full-stack frameworks like **Next.js** for web apps.

## Strengths and gaps

- **Very fluent in TypeScript** — this is my primary language and ecosystem
- **Extensive experience building AI agentic chatbots and workflows** — Vercel AI SDK, RAG, tool calling, MCP
- **Strong frontend architecture** — state management, component design, e-commerce UX
- **Backend architecture outside TS** — I lean on AI heavily here; less confident designing systems in unfamiliar ecosystems

## Work style

I enjoy architecting and implementing new technologies, providing solutions to complex business challenges, and being comfortable in the uncomfortable.

I prefer the **boy scout rule** — leave things better than you found them — but only in code I actually touched (see [[Preferences/AI Behavior]]).

## Personal

When I'm not coding: reading, photography, hiking, motorcycle riding, vinyl collecting.

I am a **Linux power user** with a strong understanding of the SDLC and the TypeScript ecosystem.
