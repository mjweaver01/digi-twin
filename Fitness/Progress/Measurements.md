# Measurements

Body measurements for the bulk — scale weight alone hides where the mass is going. Measure ~monthly, same conditions (morning, relaxed unless noted). Add columns/rows as needed; newest entries at the bottom of the table like the Weight Log.

**Progress photos:** take front/side/back monthly alongside measurements (the [[Weight Log]] already recommends this — store them wherever, just date them).

## Notes

- Waist vs chest/arm trend is the recomp signal: chest/arm up + waist flat = clean bulk
- Measure with a soft tape, same landmarks each time (widest point of arm, nipple-line chest, navel waist)
