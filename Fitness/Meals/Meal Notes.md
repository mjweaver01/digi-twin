## Food Preferences & Go-To Items

**Meals:**
- Chicken + rice (1–2 cups) with Gatorade and a handful of nuts — good training day snack/meal
- Tacos or crunchwraps: ground beef, flour tortilla, pico, sour cream, fistful of shredded cheese

- BYLT bars — 150 cal, 16g protein. Quick grab-and-go protein

**Preferred cheese:** cheese sticks or cheese cubes
