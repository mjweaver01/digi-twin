| Variation | Cal | Protein | Carbs | Fat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken + rice base | ~970 | ~57g | ~81g | ~43g |
| Swap rice → yams | ~970 | ~57g | ~81g | ~43g (similar) |
| Swap rice → salad + walnuts | ~820 | ~45g | ~20g | ~56g (fat-forward) |
| Add 1 egg | +70 | +6g | +0.5g | +5g |

**Guidance:**
- Rice/yams = carb-forward → best on training days
- Salad/walnut swap = fat-forward, lower carb → good on rest days
- Daily protein target: **155–185g**
- Daily calorie target: **~2800–3000** (300–500 surplus)
