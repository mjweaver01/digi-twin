# ME Lower

## Main Lift
Work up to a heavy single, double, or triple.
**Pick one:** Back Squat or Conventional Deadlift ← first lower session back, establish your baseline

Ramp slowly. If form breaks down, drop weight.

---

## Accessories
*Pick one option per row — original lower-body work or a shoulder swap for variation.*

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Good Mornings | 3–4 x 8–10 | Bar on back, hinge at hips |
| KB Swings | 3–4 x 15–20 | 35 lb KB, explosive hip drive |
| Bulgarian Split Squats **or** Arnold Press | 3 x 10/leg · 3–4 x 10–12 | 20/35 lb DB, rear foot on bench — or 35 lb DB press for delts |
| Reverse Hyper (band sub) **or** DB Lateral Raises | 3–4 x 15–20 · 3 x 12–15 | Face down, band kick-backs — or 20 lb DB side raises |
| Band Pull-Aparts / Face Pulls | 3–4 x 20 | Rear delt + shoulder health |
| Ab work | 3 x 10–15 | Hanging leg raises or weighted sit-ups |

**Rest:** 3–5 min on ME sets, 60–90s on accessories
