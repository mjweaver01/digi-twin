# ME Lower

## Main Lift
**Pick one:** Back Squat, Box Squat, Conventional Deadlift, Sumo Deadlift, or RDL (rotate)

**For mass, bias toward heavy 3s and 5s over true singles.** Work up to a top 3–5RM most weeks; save true singles for testing. Rotating frequently is fine, but hold a lift ~2 weeks now and then to grind a real progression.

Ramp slowly. If form breaks down, drop weight. (Watch the forward lean / bar path — brace hard, drive the upper back into the bar.)

---

## Accessories
*Pick one option per row — original lower-body work or a shoulder swap for variation.*

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Good Mornings **or** RDLs | 3–4 x 8–10 | **Load it — heavy hinge, not "light bar."** Builds the thick posterior chain |
| **Banded Leg Curls or Nordic Curls** | 3 x 10–15 | **Direct hamstring work — was missing. Add every lower day.** |
| **Back Raises / Bench Back Extensions** **or** Banded Reverse Hyper | 3–4 x 12–20 | Lower-back / reverse-hyper sub — face down over bench, band around ankles |
| Bulgarian Split Squats | 3 x 10/leg | 25/35 lb DB, rear foot on bench — quad + glute |
| KB Swings | 3–4 x 15–20 | 35 lb KB, explosive hip drive |
| Ab / bracing work | 3 x 10–15 | Hanging leg raises or weighted sit-ups — strong brace = bigger lifts |

**Rest:** 3–5 min on ME sets, 60–90s on accessories
