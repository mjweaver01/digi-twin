# How to Prompt for a New Workout

Use this in a fresh chat to generate the next session from your log. The vault already holds the program — the prompt just points at it and states the goal.

---

## Quick version

> Look at my Fitness folder — check `Program Status.md` for the pendulum week and rotation, and `Workout Log.md` for what I did last, then build today's session. Follow the conjugate structure and principles in my `Fitness Profile`. Give me the full workout, then append it to the log.

---

## Full version (paste and tweak the brackets)

> Today is [date]. Check my Fitness folder — read `Workout Log.md` for my last session and rotation spot, and follow my `Fitness Profile` + `Saved Workouts` principles. Build [today's ME/DE day], rotating the main lift and giving me the weight to beat (or the DE pendulum week + band). I'm feeling [fresh / beat up] with about [time]. Write the full session, then append it to the log.

---

## What makes it work — cover these four things

- **Which day is up** — say "today is [date]" or "I last trained [ME Upper] on [date]" so it can place you in the rotation: ME Upper → ME Lower → DE Upper → DE Lower.
- **Progression target** — ME days: "rotate the main lift and tell me the number to beat from last time that variation came up." DE days: "tell me which pendulum week I'm on (50/55/60%) plus bar weight + band."
- **How you feel / time** — one line like "beat up, 45 min" or "fresh, full session" lets it scale the volume.
- **What to do with it** — "append it to `Workout Log.md`" so it lands in the log, not just the chat.

After the session, give it a **Feel score (1–10)** when logging — trending Feel is the recovery signal that decides deloads (see `Program Status.md`).
